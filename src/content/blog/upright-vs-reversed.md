A common beginner's worry: "I drew a reversed card. Is that bad?"

No. Reversed cards aren't bad luck, and they aren't warnings. They're the same card, turned inward.

## What a reversal can mean

A reversed card can mean any of four things, depending on the card and the position:

- **Blocked** — the energy is present but stuck.
- **Delayed** — the energy is on its way but not yet here.
- **Excessive** — the energy is overdone and needs restraint.
- **Inward** — the energy is being felt privately rather than expressed.

## A useful rule

Read the upright meaning first. Then ask: what would happen if that energy turned inward? If upright
The Tower is "sudden upheaval," reversed The Tower is "the upheaval is happening internally" — the
rebuilding is already underway beneath the surface.

## When in doubt, use position

A reversed card in The Present says something about your current state. A reversed card in The Outcome
says something about where the energy is moving. Let the position narrow it down.
