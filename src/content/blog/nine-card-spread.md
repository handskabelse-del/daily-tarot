The nine-card spread is one of the most complete single-deck layouts in tarot. Unlike a three-card pull, it
maps a full arc: what is happening now, what underlies it, what's approaching, and where the current path
leads. Here's how to read it.

## The nine positions

1. **The Present** — what's happening right now, in this moment.
2. **The Challenge** — the immediate obstacle or tension you face.
3. **The Foundation** — the underlying cause or past influence.
4. **The Recent Past** — an energy that is just departing.
5. **The Crown** — the conscious goal or best possible outcome.
6. **The Near Future** — what is approaching within days or weeks.
7. **The Self** — your current attitude or inner state.
8. **The Environment** — how others or your surroundings influence you.
9. **The Outcome** — the likely resolution if the current path continues.

## Reading order

Read the cards **in order**, not in the order they catch your eye. Each position has a job. The Present and
The Challenge set up the question; The Foundation and The Recent Past give it history; The Crown and The
Near Future show the direction; The Self and The Environment are you and your world; The Outcome is the
landing.

## Look for patterns

- **Repeating suits** (three or more Wands, say) amplify that suit's message.
- **Major Arcana** in The Outcome is significant — major life themes.
- **Reversed cards** in The Self point to inner work; reversed in The Environment point to others.
- **Court cards** are often people, not energies.

## What to do next

A reading is only useful if you do something with it. After a daily reading, pick one position and one
small action. The Outcome is not a prediction — it's the path that forms from your next choice. Change
the choice, change the path.
